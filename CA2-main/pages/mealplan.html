Meal Planning Tips

Weekly Meal Planning

Plan your meals before the week starts. This helps save time, reduce stress and avoid unnecessary spending.

Create a Grocery List

Write down all the ingredients you need before shopping. A grocery list helps reduce food waste and ensures you buy only what you need.

Food Storage

Store cooked meals in airtight containers and refrigerate or freeze them properly to keep food fresh for longer.

Meal Preparation

Prepare ingredients such as vegetables, meat and sauces in advance. This makes cooking faster during busy weekdays.
