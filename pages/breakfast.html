Breakfast Recipes

Scrambled Eggs

Scrambled eggs are a quick and nutritious breakfast made with eggs, butter and seasoning. They are soft, fluffy and perfect with toast.

How to Make Scrambled Eggs

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Add a small amount of milk or cream if desired, then season with salt and pepper.
  3. Whisk the mixture until the yolks and whites are fully combined.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan over low to medium heat and melt some butter.
  5. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
  6. Allow the eggs to cook for a few seconds before gently stirring with a spatula.
  7. Continue pushing the eggs from the edges toward the centre to form soft curds.
  8. Remove the pan from the heat while the eggs are still slightly creamy.
  9. Serve immediately with toast or your favourite toppings.

French Toast

French toast is made by dipping bread into an egg mixture before frying until golden brown. It is often served with syrup, fruits or butter.

How to Make French Toast

  1. Whisk together eggs, milk, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and a little sugar in a shallow bowl.
  2. Heat a frying pan over medium heat and add butter.
  3. Dip each slice of bread into the egg mixture, coating both sides.
  4. Allow the bread to absorb the mixture for a few seconds without becoming too soggy.
  5. Place the bread onto the hot pan.
  6. Cook until the bottom is golden brown.
  7. Flip the bread and cook the other side until evenly browned.
  8. Repeat with the remaining slices.
  9. Serve warm with maple syrup, butter, fresh fruits, or powdered sugar.

Waffles

Waffles are crispy on the outside and fluffy on the inside. They can be served with maple syrup, berries or whipped cream.

How to Make Waffles

  1. Preheat the waffle maker according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  2. In a bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  3. In another bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
  5. Lightly grease the waffle maker if needed.
  6. Pour the recommended amount of batter onto the waffle plate.
  7. Close the lid and cook until the waffle is golden brown and crisp.
  8. Carefully remove the waffle using a non-metal utensil.
  9. Serve with butter, maple syrup, fruits, whipped cream, or your favourite toppings.
