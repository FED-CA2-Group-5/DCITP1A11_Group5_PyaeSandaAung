About Simply Savory

Good food doesn't have to be complicated.

Simply Savory was created by students from Singapore Polytechnic who enjoy cooking and sharing simple recipes with others.

Our mission is to inspire young adults to cook delicious meals using easy recipes and affordable ingredients.
