Chocolate Covered Strawberries

Fresh strawberries dipped in melted chocolate create a simple yet elegant dessert that is perfect for any occasion.

  • Wash and dry the strawberries thoroughly.
  • Melt the chocolate until smooth.
  • Dip each strawberry into the melted chocolate.
  • Place the strawberries on baking paper.
  • Allow the chocolate to set completely.
  • Serve immediately or chill before serving.

    • Butter Cookies

    Butter cookies are crisp, buttery and lightly sweet. They are easy to bake and perfect for sharing with family and friends.

  • Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  • Mix in the egg and vanilla extract.
  • Add the flour and mix into a soft dough.
  • Pipe or shape the dough onto a baking tray.
  • Bake until the edges are lightly golden.
  • Cool the cookies before serving.

    • Chocolate Mug Cake

    Chocolate mug cake is a rich dessert made in the microwave in just a few minutes. It is perfect when you want a quick chocolate treat.

  • Mix the dry ingredients in a microwave-safe mug.
  • Add the milk, oil, and vanilla extract.
  • Stir until the batter is smooth.
  • Microwave for about 1 to 2 minutes until cooked.
  • Let the mug cake cool slightly.
  • Serve with whipped cream or ice cream if desired.
