Chicken Caesar Wrap

A chicken Caesar wrap is a handheld version of the Caesar salad, combining grilled chicken, crisp romaine lettuce, creamy Caesar dressing, and Parmesan cheese inside a soft tortilla. It's a portable, flavorful, and satisfying meal that blends richness with freshness.

  • Texture: Soft tortilla, tender chicken, crunchy lettuce, creamy dressing, and sometimes crispy croutons.
  • Color: Golden tortilla, white chicken with brown edges, bright green lettuce, pale ivory dressing, and light yellow Parmesan.
  • Flavor: Savory chicken base, creamy garlicky dressing, fresh lettuce brightness, and salty nutty Parmesan, all balanced in one bite.
  • 1 tortilla wrap
  • 1 cup grilled chicken, sliced
  • 1 cup romaine lettuce
  • 2 tbsp Caesar dressing
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese

Chicken and Sweet Corn Soup with Noodles

Chicken and sweet corn soup with noodles is a warm, comforting dish made from tender chicken pieces, sweet corn kernels, and soft noodles simmered in a savory golden broth. It's a wholesome, filling soup that blends sweetness and savoriness in a cozy, nourishing bowl.

  • Texture: Smooth broth, juicy chicken, sweet pops of corn, and hearty noodles.
  • Color: Pale golden soup with bright yellow corn, white chicken, and creamy noodles.
  • Flavor: Savory chicken base balanced by corn’s natural sweetness, with fresh herbs often added for brightness.
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup sweet corn kernels
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g noodles
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (optional)
  • Spring onions for garnish

Carbonara

Carbonara is a rich and comforting Italian pasta dish traditionally made with spaghetti, eggs, cured pork (like guanciale or pancetta), Pecorino Romano cheese, and black pepper. It's a simple yet indulgent dish, celebrated for its balance of creaminess and bold, savory notes.

  • Texture: Silky, creamy sauce that clings to the pasta, with chewy bites of pork and a slight grain from grated cheese.
  • Color: Golden pasta coated in pale yellow sauce, speckled with crispy brown pork pieces and black pepper.
  • Flavor: Savory and smoky from the pork, nutty and salty from the cheese, with a creamy egg-based richness balanced by the sharpness of pepper
  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g pancetta or bacon
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
