Chicken Caesar Wrap

Description

Chicken Caesar Wrap is filled with grilled chicken, lettuce, Parmesan cheese and Caesar dressing wrapped in a soft tortilla. It is a quick and satisfying lunch.

How to Make

  1. Cook the chicken until fully done, then slice it into thin strips.
  2. Wash and dry the lettuce before cutting it into bite-sized pieces.
  3. In a bowl, combine the chicken, lettuce, grated Parmesan cheese, and Caesar dressing.
  4. Mix everything until the ingredients are evenly coated.
  5. Lay a tortilla flat on a clean surface.
  6. Place the chicken mixture in the centre of the tortilla.
  7. Fold in the sides and roll the tortilla tightly into a wrap.
  8. Slice the wrap in half if desired.
  9. Serve immediately or wrap it for an easy meal on the go.

Chicken and Sweet Corn Soup with Noodles

Description

This comforting soup combines tender chicken, sweet corn and noodles in a flavorful broth. It is perfect for a warm and filling meal.

How to Make

  1. Bring chicken stock to a gentle boil in a large pot.
  2. Add the chicken pieces and cook until they are fully cooked through.
  3. Remove the chicken, shred it into smaller pieces, and return it to the pot.
  4. Add the sweet corn and allow it to simmer for a few minutes.
  5. Stir in beaten eggs slowly while gently mixing the soup to create egg ribbons.
  6. Add the noodles and cook until they become tender.
  7. Season the soup with salt, pepper, and soy sauce if desired.
  8. Taste and adjust the seasoning before serving.
  9. Garnish with chopped spring onions and enjoy while hot.

Carbonara

Description

Carbonara is a classic Italian pasta made with eggs, cheese, bacon and black pepper. It is rich, creamy and easy to prepare.

How to Make

  1. Cook the pasta in boiling salted water until al dente.
  2. While the pasta cooks, fry the bacon or pancetta until crispy.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, grated Parmesan cheese, and black pepper.
  4. Reserve a small amount of pasta cooking water before draining the pasta.
  5. Add the hot pasta to the pan with the cooked bacon.
  6. Remove the pan from the heat and pour in the egg mixture.
  7. Toss the pasta quickly so the eggs create a smooth, creamy sauce.
  8. Add a little reserved pasta water if the sauce needs to be thinner.
  9. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan cheese and freshly ground black pepper.
