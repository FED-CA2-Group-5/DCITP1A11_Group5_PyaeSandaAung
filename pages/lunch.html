Chicken Caesar Wrap

Description

A Chicken Caesar Wrap is a quick and satisfying meal made with tender chicken, crisp lettuce, Parmesan cheese, and creamy Caesar dressing, all wrapped inside a soft tortilla. Beyond its convenience, this wrap balances textures and flavors: juicy chicken pairs with crunchy greens, while the salty bite of Parmesan and tangy dressing create a rich, savory profile. The tortilla keeps everything neatly contained, making it perfect for on-the-go lunches, picnics, or a light dinner. Its versatility allows for variations such as grilled chicken, whole-wheat tortillas, or added vegetables for extra nutrition. It is perfect for a quick and easy meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 tortilla wrap
  • 1 cup grilled chicken, sliced
  • 1 cup romaine lettuce
  • 2 tbsp Caesar dressing
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese

How to Make

  1. Cook the chicken until fully done, then slice it into thin strips.
  2. Wash and dry the lettuce before cutting it into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Combine chicken, lettuce, Parmesan, and Caesar dressing.
  4. Mix everything until evenly coated.
  5. Lay a tortilla flat on a clean surface.
  6. Place the chicken mixture in the centre.
  7. Fold in the sides and roll tightly.
  8. Slice the wrap in half if desired.
  9. Serve immediately or wrap for later.

Chicken and Sweet Corn Soup with Noodles

Description

Chicken and Sweet Corn Soup with Noodles is a warm and comforting dish made with tender chicken, sweet corn, silky egg ribbons, and soft noodles in a flavourful broth. The sweetness of corn balances the savory depth of chicken stock, while the egg ribbons add a delicate, velvety texture. Noodles make the soup hearty and filling, turning it into a complete meal rather than just a starter. This dish is often enjoyed during cooler weather or when seeking comfort food, as its gentle flavors and soothing warmth provide both nourishment and satisfaction. It is filling, easy to prepare, and perfect for a cozy meal.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup sweet corn kernels
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g noodles
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (optional)
  • Spring onions for garnish

How to Make

  1. Bring chicken stock to a gentle boil.
  2. Add chicken pieces and cook fully.
  3. Shred chicken and return to pot.
  4. Add sweet corn and simmer.
  5. Stir in beaten eggs slowly.
  6. Add noodles and cook until tender.
  7. Season with salt, pepper, soy sauce.
  8. Adjust seasoning before serving.
  9. Garnish with spring onions.

Carbonara

Description

Carbonara is a classic Italian pasta dish made with pasta, eggs, Parmesan cheese, black pepper, and crispy bacon or pancetta. The hot pasta creates a rich and creamy sauce without using cream, making it a simple yet delicious comfort meal. Its charm lies in the balance of flavors: the saltiness of pancetta, the sharpness of Parmesan, and the gentle heat of black pepper meld into a silky sauce that clings to each strand of pasta. Originating from Rome, carbonara embodies rustic Italian cooking — minimal ingredients, maximum flavor. When prepared correctly, it delivers a luxurious mouthfeel and a depth of taste that feels indulgent yet homely, making it a timeless favorite worldwide.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g pancetta or bacon
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste

How to Make

  1. Cook pasta until al dente.
  2. Fry bacon until crispy.
  3. Whisk eggs, Parmesan, and pepper.
  4. Reserve pasta water before draining.
  5. Add pasta to bacon pan.
  6. Remove pan from heat, add egg mix.
  7. Toss pasta quickly for creamy sauce.
  8. Add pasta water if needed.
  9. Serve with extra Parmesan and pepper.
