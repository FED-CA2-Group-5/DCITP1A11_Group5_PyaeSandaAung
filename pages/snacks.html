Healthy Snack Recipes

Honey Oat Granola Bars

These homemade granola bars are made with oats, honey and nuts. They are healthy, filling and great for busy days.

  • Mix the oats, honey, and other ingredients in a bowl.
  • Press the mixture evenly into a lined baking pan.
  • Bake until lightly golden.
  • Allow the granola to cool completely.
  • Cut into bars or squares.
  • Store in an airtight container and enjoy.

    • Air Fryer Sweet Potato Fries

    Sweet potato fries are crispy on the outside and soft inside. Cooking them in an air fryer makes them healthier and delicious.

  • Peel and cut the sweet potatoes into thin strips.
  • Toss the fries with oil and seasonings.
  • Preheat the air fryer if needed.
  • Arrange the fries in a single layer in the basket.
  • Air-fry until golden and crispy, shaking the basket halfway through.
  • Serve hot with your favourite dipping sauce.

    • Lime Honey Fruit Salad

    Fresh fruits mixed with a sweet honey and lime dressing create a refreshing and nutritious snack.

  • Wash and prepare the fresh fruits.
  • Cut the fruits into bite-sized pieces.
  • Whisk together the honey and lime juice.
  • Pour the dressing over the fruit.
  • Gently toss until the fruit is evenly coated.
  • Chill before serving for the best flavour.
