French Toast

French toast is a beloved breakfast dish made by soaking slices of bread in a mixture of beaten eggs, milk, and flavorings, then frying them until golden brown. It has a soft centre with a slightly crispy outside and is often served with syrup, fruit, whipped cream, or butter.

  • Texture: Crisp and golden on the outside, soft and custardy inside.
  • Flavor: Rich and slightly sweet, with hints of vanilla, cinnamon, or nutmeg depending on the recipe.
  • Appearance: Golden-brown slices, often dusted with powdered sugar or topped with fruit.
  • 2 slices bread
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Butter for frying
  • Syrup or fruit for serving

Waffles

Waffles are a popular breakfast (and sometimes dessert) dish made from a batter that is cooked between two patterned plates, giving them their signature grid-like surface. They are beloved for their crisp exterior and fluffy interior, and their versatility makes them a favorite worldwide. They can also be served with maple syrup, berries or whipped cream.

  • Texture: Crispy on the outside, soft and airy inside.
  • Flavor: Mildly sweet, buttery, and rich, often enhanced with toppings.
  • Appearance: Square or round with deep pockets that hold syrup, fruit, or other toppings.
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
