Chicken Parmesan

Description

Chicken Parmesan is a beloved Italian-American dish that combines crispy breaded chicken cutlets with rich tomato sauce and gooey melted cheese. The golden crust provides a satisfying crunch, while the mozzarella and Parmesan add indulgent creaminess. Served over pasta or alongside salad, it’s a hearty, comforting dish perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Season and coat the chicken with flour, egg, and breadcrumbs.
  2. Pan-fry the chicken until golden brown.
  3. Place the chicken in a baking dish.
  4. Top with tomato sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese.
  5. Bake until the cheese is melted and the chicken is fully cooked.
  6. Serve hot with pasta or a side salad.

Chicken Noodle Soup

Description

Chicken Noodle Soup is the ultimate comfort food, made with tender chicken, vegetables, noodles, and a flavorful broth. Its warmth and nourishment make it ideal for chilly evenings or when you’re feeling under the weather. The savory broth, soft noodles, and fresh herbs create a soothing, satisfying meal that feels like a hug in a bowl.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup egg noodles
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook the onions, carrots, celery, and garlic in olive oil.
  2. Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a boil.
  3. Add chicken and simmer.
  4. Add noodles and cook until tender.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs.
  6. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.

Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken

Description

Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken is a traditional dish named after its equal parts of soy sauce, sesame oil, and rice wine. The chicken is simmered with garlic, ginger, and fresh basil, creating a rich, savory, and slightly sweet flavor. Aromatic and deeply satisfying, it’s a staple of Taiwanese home cooking and pairs perfectly with steamed rice for a comforting dinner.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup sesame oil
  • 1/3 cup rice wine
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, sliced
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp sugar

Instructions

  1. Cook the ginger and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add the chicken and stir-fry until browned.
  3. Add the soy sauce, sesame oil, rice wine, and sugar.
  4. Simmer until the sauce thickens.
  5. Stir in the fresh basil leaves.
  6. Serve with steamed rice.
