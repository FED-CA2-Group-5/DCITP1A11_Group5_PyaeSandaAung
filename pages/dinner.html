Chicken Parmesan

Description

Chicken Parmesan is a popular Italian-American dish featuring crispy breaded chicken topped with rich tomato sauce and melted mozzarella and Parmesan cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is commonly served with pasta or fresh salad.

How to Make

  1. Season and coat the chicken with flour, egg, and breadcrumbs.
  2. Pan-fry the chicken until golden brown.
  3. Place the chicken in a baking dish.
  4. Top with tomato sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese.
  5. Bake until the cheese is melted and the chicken is fully cooked.
  6. Serve hot with pasta or a side salad.

Chicken Noodle Soup

Description

Chicken Noodle Soup is a comforting dish made with tender chicken, vegetables, noodles, and a flavourful chicken broth. It is warm, nourishing, and perfect for chilly days or whenever you need a simple, satisfying meal.

How to Make

  1. Cook the onions, carrots, celery, and garlic.
  2. Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a boil.
  3. Cook and shred the chicken.
  4. Add the noodles and cook until tender.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs.
  6. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.

Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken

Description

Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken is a traditional dish made with chicken, soy sauce, sesame oil, rice wine, garlic, ginger, and fresh basil. It has a rich, savoury, and slightly sweet flavour, making it a popular meal that pairs perfectly with steamed rice.

How to Make

  1. Cook the ginger and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add the chicken and stir-fry until browned.
  3. Add the soy sauce, sesame oil, and rice wine.
  4. Simmer until the sauce thickens.
  5. Stir in the fresh basil leaves.
  6. Serve with steamed rice.
