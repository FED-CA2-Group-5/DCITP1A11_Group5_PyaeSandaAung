Chicken Caesar Wrap

Description

A Chicken Caesar Wrap is a quick and satisfying meal made with tender chicken, crisp lettuce, Parmesan cheese, and creamy Caesar dressing, all wrapped inside a soft tortilla. The combination of juicy chicken, crunchy greens, and tangy dressing creates a balanced flavor profile. Perfect for picnics, school lunches, or a light dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 large tortilla wrap
  • 1 cup grilled chicken breast, sliced
  • 1 cup romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Caesar dressing
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese
  • Optional: cherry tomatoes or cucumber slices

Instructions

  1. Cook the chicken until fully done, then slice it into thin strips.
  2. Wash and dry the lettuce before cutting it into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Combine chicken, lettuce, Parmesan, and Caesar dressing.
  4. Mix everything until evenly coated.
  5. Lay a tortilla flat on a clean surface.
  6. Place the chicken mixture in the centre.
  7. Fold in the sides and roll tightly.
  8. Slice the wrap in half if desired.
  9. Serve immediately or wrap for later.

Chicken and Sweet Corn Soup with Noodles

Description

This soup is a comforting blend of tender chicken, sweet corn, silky egg ribbons, and soft noodles simmered in a flavorful broth. The sweetness of corn balances the savory depth of chicken stock, while noodles make it hearty enough for a full meal. It’s perfect for cold days or when you need something soothing and nourishing.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup cooked chicken breast, shredded
  • 1 cup sweet corn kernels
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 100g egg noodles
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Spring onions for garnish

Instructions

  1. Bring chicken stock to a gentle boil.
  2. Add chicken pieces and cook fully.
  3. Shred chicken and return to pot.
  4. Add sweet corn and simmer.
  5. Stir in beaten eggs slowly.
  6. Add noodles and cook until tender.
  7. Season with salt, pepper, soy sauce.
  8. Adjust seasoning before serving.
  9. Garnish with spring onions.

Carbonara

Description

Carbonara is a classic Roman pasta dish made with spaghetti, eggs, Parmesan cheese, black pepper, and crispy pancetta or bacon. The heat of the pasta creates a rich, creamy sauce without cream. Its charm lies in the balance of flavors: salty pancetta, sharp Parmesan, and the gentle heat of black pepper. It’s indulgent yet simple, making it a timeless comfort food.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g pancetta or bacon, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta until al dente.
  2. Fry pancetta or bacon until crispy.
  3. Whisk eggs, Parmesan, and pepper in a bowl.
  4. Reserve pasta water before draining.
  5. Add pasta to bacon pan.
  6. Remove pan from heat, add egg mixture.
  7. Toss pasta quickly for creamy sauce.
  8. Add pasta water if needed.
  9. Serve with extra Parmesan and pepper.
