Chicken Parmesan

Chicken Parmesan is a popular Italian-American dish featuring crispy breaded chicken topped with rich tomato sauce and melted mozzarella and Parmesan cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is commonly served with pasta or fresh salad.

  • Season and coat the chicken with flour, egg, and breadcrumbs.
  • Pan-fry the chicken until golden brown.
  • Place the chicken in a baking dish.
  • Top with tomato sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese.
  • Bake until the cheese is melted and the chicken is fully cooked.
  • Serve hot with pasta or a side salad.

    • Chicken Noodle Soup

    Chicken Noodle Soup is a comforting dish made with tender chicken, vegetables, noodles, and a flavourful chicken broth. It is warm, nourishing, and perfect for chilly days or whenever you need a simple, satisfying meal.

  • Cook the onions, carrots, celery, and garlic.
  • Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a boil.
  • Cook and shred the chicken.
  • Add the noodles and cook until tender.
  • Season with salt, pepper, and herbs.
  • Garnish with parsley and serve hot.

    • Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken

    Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken is a traditional dish made with chicken, soy sauce, sesame oil, rice wine, garlic, ginger, and fresh basil. It has a rich, savoury, and slightly sweet flavour, making it a popular meal that pairs perfectly with steamed rice.

  • Cook the ginger and garlic until fragrant.
  • Add the chicken and stir-fry until browned.
  • Add the soy sauce, sesame oil, and rice wine.
  • Simmer until the sauce thickens.
  • Stir in the fresh basil leaves.
  • Serve with steamed rice.
