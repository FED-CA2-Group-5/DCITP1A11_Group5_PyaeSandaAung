Chicken Parmesan

Parmesan, also known as Parmigiano-Reggiano, is a hard, aged Italian cheese prized for its rich flavor and granular texture. It's often grated over pasta, shaved into salads, or eaten in small chunks, adding bold umami character to dishes.

  • Texture: Firm, crumbly, and slightly gritty, with crystals that give a pleasant crunch.
  • Color: Pale golden to light straw-yellow.
  • Flavor: Nutty, savory, and sharp, with a salty depth and subtle sweetness that intensifies with age.
  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Chicken Noodle Soup

Parmesan, also known as Parmigiano-Reggiano, is a hard, aged Italian cheese prized for its rich flavor and granular texture. It's often grated over pasta, shaved into salads, or eaten in small chunks, adding bold umami character to dishes. It's warm, nourishing, and often enjoyed as a soothing meal.

  • Texture: Smooth broth, tender chicken pieces, chewy noodles, and soft vegetables.
  • Color: Golden broth with white chicken, pale noodles, and green or orange accents from vegetables.
  • Flavor: Mildly savory and aromatic, with the richness of chicken balanced by the freshness of herbs and vegetables.
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup egg noodles
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken

Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken (San Bei Ji) is a famous Taiwanese stir-fry dish made with chicken simmered in equal parts of soy sauce, rice wine, and sesame oil—hence the “three cups.” It's a fragrant, hearty dish that balances richness with freshness, often served sizzling hot with steamed rice.

  • Texture: Tender, juicy chicken pieces with a glossy, slightly sticky coating.
  • Color: Deep caramel-brown sauce clinging to the chicken, accented by bright green basil leaves.
  • Flavor: Bold and aromatic—savory soy depth, nutty sesame richness, slight sweetness, and the herbal freshness of basil.
  • 500g chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup sesame oil
  • 1/3 cup rice wine
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, sliced
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp sugar
